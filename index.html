12-Week Powerbuilding Plan

6'3" · 200 lb · Bench 275 · Squat ~315 · Deadlift ~320

Goal: get heavier and stronger. 6 days/week, 12 weeks, then retest and repeat.

Start here

  1. Read How it works once (RPE, training maxes, progression rules).
  2. Each gym day open that week's page and go to the day: Weeks 1–5, Weeks 6–10 (deload, then Block 2 swaps + loads), Weeks 11–12 (peak, test).
  3. Tap any exercise name to see the photos, form cues and steps.
  4. Eat ~3,900 kcal / 190 g protein, weigh daily, adjust every 2 weeks (Food).
