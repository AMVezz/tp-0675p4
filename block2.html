OHP, weeks 7–10: top set 1 × 4 at RPE 8 (start ~10 lb above the week-5 top set of 6), then 3 × 6 at 90% of the top set. Add 5 lb per week while RPE stays ≤8.5. In week 10, do 1 × 2–3 at RPE 8.5 I.

RDL, weeks 7–10: 3 × 5–6 at RPE 7–8. If the deadlift is your priority, you may replace the RDL with a paused deadlift (1 s below the knee) at 65–75% of deadlift TM for 3 × 3–4, which adds a second deadlift-specific exposure I.

Block 2 days = the Block 1 sessions with the swaps above and the loads in these tables.
