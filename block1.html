Resetting TMs after week 5 I. Calculate e1RM from the week-5 AMRAP: load × (1 + reps/30). Example: 215 × 9 on bench gives e1RM ≈ 280. The new TM is 90% of that e1RM, capped at +10 lb for bench and OHP and +15 lb for squat and deadlift. If the e1RM comes out below the current TM ÷ 0.9, keep the old TM. The Block 2 tables use projected TMs of squat 295, bench 255, deadlift 300; replace them with your calculated values.
