1RM attempt protocol I. Warm up with the bar × 10, then ~40% × 5, 55% × 3, 70% × 2, 80% × 1 and 87% × 1 of the projected max, resting 2–3 min between warm-ups and 4–6 min before attempts. Pick attempts from the projected max. Attempt 1 is ~92%, a weight you could triple. Attempt 2 is ~96–97%. Attempt 3 depends on how attempt 2 felt: +3–5% if it was RPE ≤8, +1–2% if it was RPE 9, and no third attempt if it was RPE 9.5+ or technique broke down.

These projections are inferences. As reference points, trained men in 8–12-week RPE-based programs gained roughly 10 kg on bench and 14–17 kg on squat (Helms 2018), and 7–11% on back squat (Graham & Cleather 2019).

AMRAP-test alternative I. If you prefer not to max, take ~85% of TM for as many reps as possible, stopping at RPE 9.5, and compute e1RM = load × (1 + reps/30).
